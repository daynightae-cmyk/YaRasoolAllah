# Recommended ingestion plan

P0: provider/rights/work/person registries.

P1: non-OCR OpenITI only under CC BY-NC-SA-compatible deployment; QDL Public Domain IIIF remote reading; OpenITI MSS transcriptions separate from images.

P2: OCR remains flagged; HathiTrust content stays remote until HTID/digitizer rights clear.

P3/P4: Google Books/WorldCat are enrichment; Internet Archive requires exact identity + item rights.

Hard gates: no fake readers, no fake downloads, no version-as-work inflation, no filename-derived editions, no rewriting source religious text.
