# Rights blockers

- OpenITI: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; commercial/incompatible use needs separate permission.
- Internet Archive: item-level review required.
- HathiTrust: metadata rights differ from volume content.
- Google Books: access is not redistribution permission.
- WorldCat/OCLC: entitlement/policy required for API or bulk use.
- QDL IIIF examples: Public Domain item statement verified; remote IIIF first pending derivative-rehosting policy review.
- OCR stays OCR_NEEDS_REVIEW.
- Modern translations, tafsir, children content and audio require separate rights.
