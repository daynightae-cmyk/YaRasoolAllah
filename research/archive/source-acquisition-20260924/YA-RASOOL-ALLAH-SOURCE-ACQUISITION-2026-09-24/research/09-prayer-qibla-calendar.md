# Prayer, Qibla, calendar, locations
Existing `client/src/visual-golden/services/prayer/aladhan.ts` already calls `GET https://api.aladhan.com/v1/timings/{DD-MM-YYYY}?latitude=...&longitude=...&method=...&school=...` and parses `code,data.timings,data.date.hijri,data.meta.timezone,method`; keep it. `locations.ts` has curated cities; expand with GeoNames `https://secure.geonames.org/searchJSON?name_startsWith=<term>&featureClass=P&maxRows=10&username=<name>`, `timezoneJSON?lat=...&lng=...&username=...`. Use a backend proxy for credentials/rate limiting and explicit city selection. Their published free quota: 10,000 credits daily, 1,000 hourly; individual service costs can differ. https://www.geonames.org/export/ ; https://www.geonames.org/export/web-services.html .

OSMF public Nominatim explicitly forbids autocomplete, caps 1 request/sec site-wide, demands identifiable User-Agent/Referer and attribution. Use **only deliberate single search** or licensed/self-hosted alternative, never as keystroke autocomplete. https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/ .

Existing `prayer/qibla.ts` implements initial great-circle bearing with Kaaba coordinates (21.422487,39.826206). Formula: `atan2(sin(Δλ)cosφ₂, cosφ₁sinφ₂−sinφ₁cosφ₂cosΔλ)` normalized [0,360). Bearing from true north ≠ device orientation; sensor heading requires separate permission, calibration, magnetic declination/true north conversion. Test Abu Dhabi, London, Cairo, and Kaaba-nearby degenerate case; compare with AlAdhan Qibla API, tolerating rounding only. https://aladhan.com/qibla-api .

AlAdhan calendar https://aladhan.com/islamic-calendar-api offers conversion and Hijri calendar based on Umm al-Qura; sample URLs must be validated before coding new calendar endpoint. Treat calculated dates as provider method, local moon sighting may change observance; white days derived from selected Hijri calendar and flagged calculated. Cache prayer responses keyed by day, rounded lat/lng, method, school and high-latitude setting; stale data must show date and provider. No invented time on failure.
