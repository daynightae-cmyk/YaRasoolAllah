# Images, manuscript scans, video
Commons MediaWiki: `GET https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php?action=query&format=json&prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata&iiurlwidth=640&titles=File:<encoded name>` gives `pages[].imageinfo[].url,thumburl,extmetadata`. For discovery use `generator=search&gsrsearch=<term>&gsrnamespace=6`, then inspect each file page and credit/license fields. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:API/MediaWiki .

LOC search and item JSON as in library file, plus image services https://www.loc.gov/apis/micro-services/image-services/ . QDL IIIF per item; Internet Archive `metadata/{identifier}` and public item player. Wikimedia Commons video can use original item's media URL only after MIME and rights checks. Do not publish generalized image URLs or attribution assembled from an unrelated asset. Record `canonicalPage,creator,credit,license,licenseUrl,modifications,iiifManifest,imageService` per item.

YouTube: `GET https://www.googleapis.com/youtube/v3/search?part=snippet&type=video&videoEmbeddable=true&q=<term>&maxResults=10&key=<key>`; `id.videoId`, `snippet.channelId,title,thumbnails`, `nextPageToken`. Then `videos?part=contentDetails,status,snippet&id=<ids>&key=<key>` for duration (ISO-8601), captions flag and status. Embed `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/<validated-video-id>` with iframe allow/fullscreen, title; validate embeddability at display time. `search.list` costs 100 quota units in official docs; avoid repeated frontend search. Docs https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/search/list ; https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/list ; https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters . Captions availability metadata is not permission to download transcript. https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/captions .
