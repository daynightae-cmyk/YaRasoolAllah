# Recommended Ingestion Plan

## P0 metadata
HathiTrust CC0 metadata contributions, documented BnF open-licence metadata datasets, and LOC public structured metadata while retaining provenance/rights fields.

## P1 machine-readable text
OpenITI 2025.1.9 only where the product is compatible with CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Keep work, edition statement, digital version and provenance separate.

## P2 remote institutional reading
Use Princeton, British Library, QDL, LOC and Gallica IIIF/external viewers while item rights are reviewed.

## P3 reconciliation
Google Books and WorldCat identifiers/metadata under their terms.

## P4/P5
Unclear Archive.org packages, community datasets, modern copyrighted editions and social leads.

Do not bulk-download the corpus yet. Ingest metadata and rights gates first.
