# Rights Blockers — Ya Rasool Allah ﷺ

- OpenITI: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; commercial deployment is not automatically cleared.
- Internet Archive: public download != rights grant; item/edition review required.
- HathiTrust: metadata is the easy layer; volume content is HTID/rightscode/digitizer/territory-specific.
- Google Books: accessInfo/preview/download signals are not redistribution rights.
- WorldCat/OCLC: API/bulk use needs entitlement/permission.
- QDL / British Library / Princeton / LOC manuscript images: item-level or collection-level rights gate required.
- No audio item is promoted without work-level rights proof.
- OCR is never presented as verified critical Qur'an/Hadith text.
