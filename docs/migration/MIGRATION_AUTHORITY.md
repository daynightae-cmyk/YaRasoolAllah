## Religious-content integrity rules
1. Do not fabricate Quran verses, hadith, authenticity grades, narrations, dates, or scholarly claims.
2. Every hadith/narration feature must support source metadata and grading/provenance where available.
3. AI answers must cite their source material and clearly distinguish sourced text from generated explanation.
4. Do not simulate, generate, or depict the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ as a person, face, body, avatar, or voice.
5. Use maps, calligraphy, architecture, manuscripts, landscapes, timelines, objects, and abstract light motifs instead.
6. Unsourced or placeholder religious content must be visibly marked for editorial review, not silently presented as fact.
